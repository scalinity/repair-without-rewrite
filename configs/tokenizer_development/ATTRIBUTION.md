# DEVELOPMENT tokenizer lineage and attribution

This locally trained byte-BPE artifact derives from admitted training `text_raw` of LibriSpeech-PC, OpenSLR145 (https://www.openslr.org/145/), by Aleksandr Meister, Matvei Novikov, Nikolay Karpov, Evelina Bakhturina, Vitaly Lavrukhin and Boris Ginsburg, “LibriSpeech-PC: Benchmark for Evaluation of Punctuation and Capitalization Capabilities of end-to-end ASR Models” (https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02943). OpenSLR labels the release CC BY4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The transformation is deterministic byte-pair learning; no full source rows or audio payloads are redistributed here. See the hash-bound corpus manifest and DEVELOPMENT tokenizer qualification report. This is a DEVELOPMENT artifact, not a frozen paper protocol/tokenizer.
