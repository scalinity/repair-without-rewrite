## F01 — direct full-versus-compact ASR predecessor

Hao Zhang, Felix Stahlberg and Shankar Kumar's [Predicting Compact Phrasal Rewrites with Large Language Models for ASR Post Editing, arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.13831v1) compares full transcription, numerical-span edits, source/target phrase pairs and target-only phrase replacements. Compact outputs expand deterministically by string matching/replacement with contextual dilation/anchors and tie rules. PaLM 2 Gecko/Otter are pretrained decoder-only models fine-tuned on LibriSpeech with streaming Google USM one-best hypotheses; DEV selects configurations. Table II reports WER and average output length: full 2.7/6.2 WER and 20/18 output tokens; span 3.4/7.5 and 4/5; phrase pair 3.0/7.1 and 7/10; target-only 3.0/6.8 and 6/8, for test-clean/test-other. Source WER is 6.6/11.4. Target-only dilation 3 recovers 99.8% of training references; phrase-pair dilation 1 recovers 99.98%. It reports length reduction, not a directly measured full native request-latency comparison. The [Google ICASSP 2025 listing](https://research.google/conferences-and-events/google-at-icassp-2025/) independently confirms authors/title and conference presentation.

The fetched arXiv HTML is pinned to v1: 100,613 bytes, SHA256 `e1f41d652744f8eb713c123dfcd3637a11646364c271129cc03a5918627b8e2c`, fetched `2026-10-05T05:02:17.455939+00:00`. Google listing: 180,429 bytes, SHA256 `72b53e6d6bc3e2e29e172b606dc7e953b098e026656b3a21e6ab1dcc3b9363ef`.

**Positioning inference:** the prior directly overlaps the comparison of full output versus compact ASR rewrites plus deterministic expansion. “First full-versus-compact ASR experiment” is unsupported and excluded. The proposed remaining distinction is B100/C101 on a shared random-init backbone, nearly matched parameters and common canonical exposure, with edit/gap eligibility, repaired and introduced reference-error units, ambiguity bounds, constitutive renderer failures and full native system cost. Those are prospective experimental distinctions, not established novelty, superiority, or results. Compact output length alone does not establish faster full requests; this foundation has made no PaLM/USM reproduction.
